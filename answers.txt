Q1a. Agile Pros/Cons:
Pros:
1.Dynamic changes are possible (not possible in other methodologies such as waterfall) 
2. Customer value is created in each iteration rather than only at the very end of the development cycle.
Cons: 
1. Due to the iterative nature of agile development, more extensive documentation is required to keep track of all the changes that occur in each iteration. 
2. The point when a project is complete or finished is less defined as teams always have the option to keep iterating, which could possibly create unnecessary work as teams are unsure about when to stop working on a particular task. 

Q1b. Role and responsibilities of scrum master vs product owner: 
Scrum master: The scrum master is the person who handles all the scrum related work, such as managing meetings (both cross team meetings and smaller stand ups) and sprints, ensuring Jira accurately reflects the state of the project, and generally handles tasks related to the scrum implementation of agile development.
Product Owner: The product owner is the one who sets the direction for the project itself, handling what the future of the project is, along with dealing with stakeholders and what official releases of the product are actually made.

Q1c. 3 types of meetings:
The 3 types of meetings that occur during a sprint cycle are the Sprint Planning meetings, daily Stand-up meetings, and Sprint Retrospective meetings. The Sprint Planning meetings occur before sprints themselves; they outline what work will be finished in the sprint, determining the complexity of the tasks to be done, and setting the sprint up in Jira (assigning story points, moving stories from backlog to the upcoming sprint, etc). The daily Stand-up meetings occur during a sprint, which are daily, short (10-15 mins) meetings where members of teams briefly meet to discuss work completed the previous day, their plan for what to complete that day, and any potential difficulties/blockers. The Sprint Retrospective meeting occurs at the end/after a sprint has been finished, discussing what work was completed, and moving anything that was not finished back into the backlog to be completed in a future sprint. The sprint is also closed in Jira.
